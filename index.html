About

I had coaches who used data to develop me. Now I try to be that coach.

I'm a hitting trainer at Driveline Baseball in Tampa, Florida. Before that, I was the hitting coach at Dakota State University in Madison, South Dakota.

Most of my life is dedicated to furthering athletes' careers and helping them understand what they need to do to get to the next level. Early in my college career I was fortunate to have coaches who used data and technology to develop players. Those experiences are a big part of why I use the same resources in my own coaching.

At Driveline I've had the chance to put my creativity to the test and build tools that help both players and coaches. This site is a home for everything I make and my thoughts on offensive development. It will keep changing as I keep improving in my career. Hopefully someone finds it useful, interesting, or both.

Selected work

Tools I've built

Things I've made for players and coaches. Most of them are live, so click through and try them.

Hitting Tracker

01

Game day / Live

Built for the Mount Marty staff. One coach charts the game pitch by pitch, and every hitter page, team page and export updates from that same data. Swing decisions included.

Open toolGitHub

Bat Path Explainer

02

Coach education / Live

A walkthrough of the bat path, written for coaches. Is the barrel working up or down, side to side, how fast, and how adjustable? Every swing is rebuilt in 3D from 330,422 tracked swings in the 2025 MLB season.

Open toolGitHub

VAA Matcher

03

Research / Live

Lines up every 2025 MLB hitter's attack angle with the slope of the pitches he sees. When the two don't match, that's one reason a hitter struggles with certain pitch types. The Plane Match Score puts a number on it, 0 to 100.

Open tool18 MB, best on desktop

Hitter Dev Report

04

Player development / Single-file tool

Drop in a hitter's Blast and HitTrax files and get one report, compared to whatever level you pick, that prints as a PDF the athlete can take home. Add your program's logo and the report picks up its colors.
