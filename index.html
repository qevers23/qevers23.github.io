Mount Marty Hitter Tracker

Pitch-by-pitch tracking and swing-decision reports for Mount Marty hitters. A coach charts the game live, and every hitter page, team page and export updates from the same shared data.

  • Live game charting, hitter, team, games and roster views
  • Zone profile by pitch density, swing rate, miss rate, exit velo, expected damage and decision runs
  • Filter by games or scrimmages, count, pitch type, pitcher hand and date
  • One link for the whole staff, with a free-tier hosted database behind it

Teaching / coach education

Bat Path Explainer

An interactive 3D explainer for coaches on attack angle, swing tilt, attack direction, bat speed and getting on plane. Each concept pairs a swing you can orbit and scrub with where MLB hitters actually live.

  • Five camera views (3/4, side, catcher, top, low) and a play-through to contact
  • Sliders for attack angle, swing tilt, swing length and pitch height
  • MLB 10th, median and 90th percentile for every metric, from 2025 pitch-level Statcast bat tracking
  • "Where coaches get this wrong" notes tied to common cues

Player development / reports

Hitter Dev Report

One page that turns a hitter's Blast and HitTrax exports into a full development report and prints as a PDF you can hand the athlete. It replaces a three-tool workflow: swing profile in one, batted ball in another, comparison numbers in a third.

  • Drag in Blast per-swing CSV, HitTrax CSV and Driveline Snapshot or Swing Profile PDFs, in any order
  • Swing profile, batted ball and point-of-contact sections compared against a chosen playing level
  • Drop in a program logo and the report takes on its colors
  • Runs from a single HTML file with nothing to install

Research / MLB 2025

VAA Matcher

Every 2025 MLB hitter's attack angle against the vertical approach angle of the pitches he faces. Plane mismatch is a mechanical explanation for why some hitters struggle with some pitch types.

  • Five views: 3D success ridge, VAA x attack angle scatter, violins by pitch type, gap-to-outcome curves and a zone map
  • Plane Match Score, a 0-100 score for how closely a hitter's attack angle tracks the league's xwOBA-optimal angle for each pitch's VAA and height
  • Each hitter compared against hitters in the same bat-speed group
